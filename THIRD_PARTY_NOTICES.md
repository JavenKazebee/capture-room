## GStreamer framework

`Contents/Frameworks/GStreamer.framework` is the official GStreamer macOS runtime,
unmodified apart from unused packages being left out (`scripts/macos/gstreamer.sh`). It is
built by [Cerbero](https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/gstreamer/cerbero) at the tag matching
its version (`GST_VERSION` in `.github/workflows/release.yml`). Each Cerbero recipe names
the exact upstream source it builds.

Most of it is LGPL (GStreamer itself, FFmpeg built as LGPL, LAME, librtmp and others) or
permissive. These libraries in it are **GPL-2.0-or-later**, and are why Capture Room as a
whole is distributed under the GPL:

- **x264**: software H.264 encoding (`x264enc`)
- **x265**: software H.265 encoding (`x265enc`)
- **a52dec**: AC-3 audio decoding (`a52dec`)

The LGPL libraries are dynamically linked and can be replaced in the bundle with
compatible builds of your own.

### Source code

The complete source of Capture Room is at <https://github.com/JavenKazebee/capture-room>,
including the scripts that assemble the app.

The source of every GPL and LGPL library in a release is the upstream release named in
that release's `GStreamer/versions.txt`, built by the Cerbero tag of the same GStreamer
version: GStreamer publishes Cerbero and its recipes at
<https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/gstreamer/cerbero>, and each recipe points to its
project's source. If you can't find the source for a release, open an issue at
<https://github.com/JavenKazebee/capture-room/issues>.

## Built into the binary

These are compiled into `capture-room` itself rather than shipped as libraries. Their
license texts are in `rust-crates.txt`.

- **gst-plugins-rs** (MPL-2.0): the NDI, fallbackswitch and WebRTC (WHIP/WHEP) plugins, at
  the revision pinned in `node/Cargo.toml`. Source:
  <https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/gstreamer/gst-plugins-rs>.
- **gstreamer-rs** (MIT or Apache-2.0) and the other Rust crates, under permissive
  licenses (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD, ISC, Zlib, Unicode-3.0 and similar). `about.toml` lists
  the licenses accepted, so a dependency under any other license fails the build.

The web UI embedded in the binary uses Vue and other MIT, ISC and Apache-2.0 packages
(`ui-packages.txt`), and the **Inter** and **JetBrains Mono** typefaces under the SIL Open
Font License 1.1.

by patents in some countries. The licenses above cover copyright, not patents, and no
patent license is given for these formats. Whether you need one depends on where and how
you use them.
